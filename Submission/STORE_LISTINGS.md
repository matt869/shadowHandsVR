## Store text (copy and paste)

**Title:** Shadow Hands

**Short description (one line):**
Make hand shadows on a lamp-lit wall. Match the silhouette and it comes alive. Hands only.

**Description:**
> Hand-shadow puppets are the oldest game people play with light and a wall. Shadow Hands brings them
> into VR with your real, tracked hands.
>
> Sit at a desk under a single lamp. A faint silhouette appears on the wall: a rabbit, a wolf, a
> dragon. Shape your hands until your shadow matches it. The closer you get, the darker the target
> grows. Hold the match for a moment and the shadow comes alive.
>
> - 10 creatures, from one-hand animals to two-hand shapes
> - A new Daily Shadow every day, with a streak
> - A 20-second tutorial on first launch
> - Hands only: no controllers, no locomotion, play seated
> - The score is physical: the game measures the real shadow on the wall, so any hand shape that
>   casts the right silhouette counts
>
> **How to play:**
> - **VR:** open https://matt869.github.io/shadowHandsVR/ in the Meta Quest Browser and press
>   **Enter VR**, or install the APK.
> - **No headset?** Open the same link on a laptop and press **Play with your webcam**. Your camera
>   never leaves your device.
>
> This is an early release. If a shape is too hard to make with your hands, please leave a comment
> saying which one.

**Tags:** vr, webxr, hand-tracking, webcam, meta-quest, puzzle, shadows, relaxing, seated
**Genre:** Puzzle

## itch.io (about 10 minutes)
1. Sign up at **itch.io** (free) and confirm your email.
2. Click your name (top right), then **Upload new project**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Title:** Shadow Hands. **Short description:** from above.
   - **Classification:** Games. **Kind of project:** **HTML**.
   - **Release status:** In development (switch it later if you like).
   - **Pricing:** *No payments*.
4. **Uploads:**
   - Upload `shadow_hands_web.zip` and tick **"This file will be played in the browser"**.
   - Upload `ShadowHands.apk` and tick **Android**.
5. **Embed options:** viewport **1280 × 720**, and tick the **fullscreen button**.
   *Note:* VR and the webcam may not start inside the itch.io page frame, because itch.io controls that
   frame's permissions. That's why the description tells players to open the GitHub link directly.
6. **Details:** paste the description, genre and tags. Add `cover_630x500.png` as the cover, the
   `still_*.jpg` files as screenshots, and the YouTube link as the trailer.
7. **Visibility:** *Public*, then **Save**. Send me the page link and I'll check it.

## SideQuest (Quest store outside Meta)
1. Sign up at **sidequestvr.com** (free), then open the developer / **Create App** area.
2. Upload `ShadowHands.apk`. The package name is `com.matt869.shadowhands`, version 1.0.
3. Icon `icon_512.png`, screenshots `still_*.jpg`, video: your YouTube link.
4. Paste the description and tags. Mark it **hand tracking supported**, controllers not needed,
   category **Game / Puzzle**, comfort **Comfortable** (seated, no movement).
5. Submit. SideQuest reviews new listings before they go public, which can take a few days.
